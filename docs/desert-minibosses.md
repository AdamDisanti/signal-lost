# Desert mini-bosses: the Chop Shop

The three mini-bosses of the Desert / Dunes biome. When the ship broke up, the Technology-Enhanced Hermit Crab (the biome boss) took the Engine Core, and these three took the parts bolted around it. Each one has built its stolen part into its body and fights with it. Defeating all three is meant to be the design document's "three mini encounters before the boss is scannable". Owner: Lawson. All models are low-poly placeholders generated by the builder script, and all numbers are placeholders to tune.

| Mini-boss | Stolen part | How it uses it | Fodder around it | Desert site |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Burnout**, thorny-devil lizard | Rocket thruster (the crash site's own `RocketEngine` model) | Thruster dashes that leave fire | Hubcap Armadillos + mortar frogs | Mesa Pass |
| **Old Buzzard**, vulture | Wing panels (the crash site's bent hull panels) | Flies, dive-bombs, drops scrap | Dish Meerkats + Spark Ticks | Wreck Field |
| **Sludge**, giant scorpion | Fuel tank and hose | Sprays oil, then sets it on fire | Jerry-Can Beetles + Snorkel Eels | Buried Hull |

This builds on the [desert fodder](desert-fodder.md) branch and lives entirely in new files, apart from small changes to the fodder scripts listed under [Changes to the fodder code](#changes-to-the-fodder-code). It does not touch the tutorial, the Desert Dunes scene, the crash-site assets it borrows, the shared player, scanner or HUD prefabs, or the crab and frog.

## Open the delivered assets

The local `tutorial-zone` integration now contains the **saved mini-boss prefabs, their meshes/materials, all fodder assets and all three test scenes**. Open `Assets/Scenes/Bosses/BurnoutTest.unity`, `OldBuzzardTest.unity` or `SludgeTest.unity` and press Play. The prefabs are under `Assets/Prefabs/Enemies/Desert/MiniBosses/`. No generator needs to be run over the delivered scenes, and the tutorial and Desert Dunes scene have not been populated with new enemies.

## Create the prefabs and test scenes

For a checkout containing only scripts, or reproduction in a disposable project:

1. Open the `signal-lost/` project in Unity **6000.6.2f1** with `feature/desert-minibosses` merged and wait for scripts to compile.
2. Choose **Signal Lost > Desert Mini-Bosses > Create Test Scenes**.
3. The Console should print `DESERT_MINIBOSS_TEST_SCENES_CREATED_AND_VALIDATED`.

It creates:

- `Assets/Prefabs/Enemies/Desert/MiniBosses/`: `Burnout`, `OldBuzzard` and `Sludge` prefabs. Their meshes go in `Assets/Prefabs/Enemies/Desert/Meshes/`.
- `Assets/Materials/Enemies/Desert/`: their materials.
- Any missing desert fodder prefabs.
- `Assets/Scenes/Bosses/BurnoutTest.unity`, `OldBuzzardTest.unity` and `SludgeTest.unity`.

Scenes that already exist are skipped, never overwritten. **Regenerate Prefabs** rebuilds the three prefabs with the same GUIDs, throwing away edits made to them. **Validate** re-runs the checks. Commit everything with its `.meta` files.

Each test scene is a 64 x 64 m walled sand arena themed on the boss's site:

- Mesa Pass has four mesas to crash into and bank off.
- Wreck Field has a ring of low hull plates.
- Buried Hull has a half-buried hull to the north.

The mini-boss patrols near the middle. You start at the south edge, outside its 20 m arena. The player has the same setup as the Desert Dunes level: laser, 100 HP, placeholder respawn, scanner on **Q**, and HUD.

**To fight a mini-boss alone**, select it in the scene and untick **Fodder While Engaged**.

## How every mini-boss works

- **Patrol:** it walks a loop near its home (Old Buzzard circles high overhead).
- **Engaged:** walking into its 20 m arena, or hitting it, starts the fight.
  - A boss health bar appears at the top of the screen, with a notch where phase 2 starts.
  - Its fodder spawner starts sending waves at the arena's edge, preferring spots behind you, with a cap on how many are alive at once.
- **Phase 2 at half health:** the stolen part breaks. A banner says so, the boss pauses for a moment, and then it fights differently.
- **Leash and reset:** leave the arena for 5 s, or die, and it resets: full health, phase 1, back home, fodder cleared.
- **Defeat:**
  - Spawning stops; fodder already out keeps fighting.
  - The stolen part drops as a glowing, spinning pickup. Walk up to it: **RECOVERED: … (n/3)**.
- **Spotted:** if a Dish Meerkat spots you, the mini-bosses notice you from anywhere in their arena and move faster. Old Buzzard also dives twice as often.

## Burnout (rocket thruster)

| Phase | Move | What happens | Counter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Thruster dash** | The nozzle glows and it crouches for 1 s. It aims for the first 60%, then the direction locks. It rockets up to 30 m in a straight line (24 damage and a shove), leaving a streak of fire (6 damage per half second). | Sidestep after the lock. |
| 1 | **Overshoot** | A dodged dash skids and spins out, then it is dizzy for 1.4 s. | Your opening. |
| 1 | **Crash** | Dashing into a mesa or wall stuns it for 2.2 s. | A bigger opening. |
| 1, 2 | **Tail whip** | Close in front or beside it: 0.55 s crouch, then a full spin with the tail (16 damage). | Back off on the crouch. |
| 1, 2 | **Exhaust blast** | Close behind it: the nozzle flares for 0.6 s, then a cone of fire blasts backwards (20 damage, a shove, fire left behind). | Don't hug its back. |
| 2 | **Chain dash** | Shorter wind-ups (0.7 s, then 0.4 s re-aims). It chains three sputtering dashes and banks off walls. | Keep moving; watch each re-aim. |
| 2 | **Overheat** | After a chain it stalls for 3 s, smoking, and takes **1.6x damage**. | Unload on it. |

420 HP.

## Sludge (fuel tank)

| Phase | Move | What happens | Counter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Oil spray** | The tail curls forward and the tank gurgles for 0.8 s, then it lobs 3 globs. The first lands on you, the rest scatter around you, each marked by a ring. Each leaves a slick for 14 s that **slows you by half**. | Move out of the rings. |
| 1 | **Ignite** | When you are near one of its slicks, the blue pilot light on its tail flickers hot for 0.6 s, then it sparks the slick into fire (9 damage per half second). It never lights a slick it's standing in. | Leave the oil when the igniter flickers. |
| 1, 2 | **Claw snap** | Close in front: pincers open for 0.5 s, then snap twice (12 each). | Back off on the open. |
| 2 | **Punctured tank** | Fuel gushes into four slicks around it. It leaves a burning trail as it walks and sprays 5 globs, more often. | |

440 HP. Ways to hurt it:

- **Backfire:** a heavy hit (20+, a charged shot or the slam) on the **fuel tank** while it is winding up or spraying. The tank explodes on its back (it takes 70, and anything near it 22), and it is stunned for 3.2 s lying in burning fuel.
- **Your fires hurt it:** fires it starts don't hurt it, but fires you start do. Shoot a slick it is standing in.
- **Chain reactions:** beetle blasts light slicks too, and fire hurts fodder, so lure them into the flames.

## Shared systems added

- **`DesertMiniBoss`:** the base for all three. It handles patrol, engage, the boss bar, phase 2, leash/reset, fodder control, the part drop, and a weak-spot relay. `DesertMiniBoss.Defeated` and `DefeatedCount` are there for a future encounter counter or boss unlock; nothing uses them yet.
- **`MiniBossHud`:** **PLACEHOLDER** IMGUI boss bar, banners and sand-blind overlay. The shared HUD prefab is untouched.
- **`ShipPartDrop`:** the dropped part pickup. **PLACEHOLDER**: it only records the part's name (`ShipPartDrop.Recovered`). There is no inventory or ship repair yet.
- **`FireZone` / `FireStyle`:** ground fire that hurts every `Health` inside it once per tick and ignites oil and beetles. It can be immune to whoever started it.
- **`OilSlick`, `OilGlob`, `PlayerSlowReceiver`:**
  - Oil puddles slow the player by undoing part of each frame's movement, without changing `FirstPersonController`.
  - Oil burns when fire, a blast or a laser bolt touches it.
- **`ScrapBomb`, `GroundMarker`:** falling scrap, and the warning rings and shadows.

## Changes to the fodder code

- **Bug fix (Snorkel Eel):** `Health` reports the damage it actually applied, capped at its current HP. The eel's 1 HP snorkel hitbox therefore always reported 1 damage, so shooting the pipe did almost nothing. The snorkel now has a huge, constantly restored pool, like the mini-bosses' weak spots. If you already generated the fodder prefabs, run **Signal Lost > Desert Fodder > Regenerate Prefabs** to pick this up. The same thing affects the mortar frog's tin can (Christian's code, not changed here): hitting the can when the frog isn't loading passes on 1 damage instead of the hit's damage.
- **`FodderSpawner`:**
  - New `Clear()`, so a resetting mini-boss can remove its fodder.
  - Dead enemies without their own cleanup (crabs, frogs) are now removed 6 s after dying.
- **`DesertFodderBuilder`:** some helpers are now `internal` so the mini-boss builder can share them, and a new `EnsureFodderPrefabs()`.
- **Spark Tick:** its idle shuffle no longer depends on the NavMeshAgent's remaining distance before it has a path.

## Manual acceptance checks

For each scene: press Play. No Console errors (a short pause while the NavMesh builds is normal).

**All three**

1. The mini-boss patrols near the middle and ignores you at the start. Walk north into its arena: the boss bar appears, and fodder starts arriving after about 2 s, from behind you where possible.
2. Walk back out past the arena edge and wait 5 s: the bar disappears, the boss is back home at full health, and the fodder is gone.
3. Get it below half health: a "DAMAGED" banner, a short pause, then phase 2. The bar turns red and shows "(enraged)".
4. Kill it: the "DEFEATED" banner. Fodder stops coming, and the glowing part floats near the body. Walk into the part: **RECOVERED: … (1/3)**.
5. Die during a fight: you respawn at the south edge, and the boss has reset.

**Burnout**

1. The nozzle glows and it crouches before every dash. Strafe late: it misses, skids, spins and wobbles dizzy. A fire streak follows its path and hurts you if you stand in it, but never hurts Burnout.
2. Lure it into a mesa: it crashes and lies stunned.
3. Stand close in front: tail whip. Stand close behind: the exhaust blast shoots fire back.
4. Phase 2: the engine is tilted and dark and smokes. It chains three sputtering dashes, banks off walls, then "OVERHEATED!": it stalls, smoking, and takes visibly more damage.

**Old Buzzard**

1. It circles high and can't be reached with melee. A shadow grows under you: move once it stops following you, and the dive misses. It sits on the ground for a moment; hit it.
2. A bombing run drops scrap in a line, with red rings first.
3. A charged shot on a wing panel makes it wobble and drop out of its attack.
4. Phase 2: one wing (the one you hit more) tears off and falls, and the bird crashes.
   - It walks lopsided.
   - Bolts from the front glance off.
   - It pecks and hop-stomps.
   - The wing gust shoves you and fills the screen with sand.
   - Now and then it tries to fly, fails, and sits dazed.
5. When it dies, the torn wing on the ground becomes the pickup.

**Sludge**

1. The tail curls and the tank shakes, then three globs land around you with rings, leaving dark puddles. Standing in a puddle slows you.
2. Near a puddle, the pilot light flickers, sparks fly, and the puddle bursts into flame. Fire spreads to touching puddles.
3. Shoot a puddle it is standing in: it catches fire and Sludge takes damage.
4. Charge a shot and hit the yellow tank while it is curling or spraying: **BACKFIRE!**. A blast, a big chunk of health gone, and it lies on its side in fire for about 3 s.
5. Phase 2: oil gushes around it, it leaves burning patches as it walks, and it sprays five globs.
6. Beetles that wander into the fire chain-explode.

  - Should the boss become scannable after all three are beaten?
  - `Defeated`, `DefeatedCount` and `ShipPartDrop.Recovered` are ready for this, but nothing is wired up.

- **Death and reset rules:** the mini-bosses reset when you die or leave. Whether progress such as a recovered part survives a death isn't decided.
- **Frogs:** Burnout's arena reuses Christian's mortar frog prefab unchanged, so the tin-can damage note above applies there.
