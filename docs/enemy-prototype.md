# Enemy prototype: hermit crab

Sequence step 2 from the [work guidelines](WORK_GUIDELINES_README.md), second half: an enemy the mining laser can damage, and that damages the player. The [combat prototype](combat-prototype.md) covers the laser.

The enemy is a goofy low-poly hermit crab that wears a swappable shell. Its first shell is a metal bucket. All models are placeholders generated by the builder script; no third-party assets are used.

## Create the test scene (once)

1. Open the project in Unity **6000.6.2f1** on the `feature/basic-enemy` branch and wait for scripts to compile. The Console should have no red errors.
2. Choose **Signal Lost > Create Enemy Test Scene** from the top menu bar.
3. The Console should print `ENEMY_TEST_SCENE_CREATED_AND_VALIDATED`, and `Assets/Scenes/EnemyTest.unity` opens.

The menu refuses to run again once the scene exists. It also creates, the first time:

- `Assets/Prefabs/Enemies/HermitCrab.prefab`: the crab.
- `Assets/Prefabs/Enemies/Shells/MetalBucket.prefab`: the bucket shell.
- `Assets/Prefabs/Enemies/Meshes/`: the low-poly meshes, and `Assets/Materials/Enemies/`: their materials.

Commit all of these, with their `.meta` files, with this feature. **Signal Lost > Regenerate Hermit Crab Prefabs** rebuilds the crab, bucket and meshes from the builder (keeping their GUIDs) if the builder changes; it overwrites edits made to those prefabs.

The scene's player is the shared `FirstPersonPlayer` prefab with the mining laser, a 100 HP **Health**, the debug HUD, and **PlaceholderRespawn** added in this scene only. The shared prefab is not changed.

## Run it

Open `Assets/Scenes/EnemyTest.unity`, press Play, and click the Game view. Controls are unchanged: see the [movement](movement-prototype.md) and [combat](combat-prototype.md) prototypes.

The arena has two crabs. **Hermit Crab A** waits ahead of you; **Hermit Crab B** is further back on the left. There is cover, a pillar to hide behind, and a ramp up to a platform. The HUD shows your health in the top left and the crab under the crosshair.

## How the crab behaves (provisional)

The design document asks for idle, chase, attack and dead states. Everything else here is a placeholder choice to playtest.

- **Idle:** ambles around near where it started. If it sees you within 12 m (line of sight, walls block it), it jumps in surprise, eyes popping, and starts chasing.
- **Chase:** follows you around obstacles on the NavMesh while **keeping its eyes and claws turned toward you**, side-stepping like a crab when the path bends. If it loses sight of you it faces where it is walking. It is slower than your walk, so you can back off. (Turn off **Face Player While Chasing** for a sideways scuttle instead.) It gives up if you get more than 18 m away or stay out of sight for 3 s.
- **Attack:** when close, it **raises its big claw**, which **glows** from orange to blazing yellow-white, with a spark between the pincers that pulses faster as the strike nears. That 0.9 s wind-up is your warning. It keeps aiming at you only for the first half; after that its aim is locked, so a late **sidestep or backstep** makes it miss. Then it lunges and slams the claw down. If you are still in reach and within the 90° in front of it, you take 15 damage and get shoved back.
- **Missed slam:** the claw is driven deep into the ground with a burst of dirt, and **gets stuck**. For about 1.2 s the crab struggles: it heaves back on the buried claw over and over (kicking up dirt each time), trembles, scrabbles its legs for grip, bulges its eyes and hammers the ground with its small claw. Then it rips the claw free in a spray of dirt and the pull throws it about 0.8 m backwards: it rears up, wobbles, backpedals and bounces, eyes spinning (1.6 s in total, plus the stumble) before it can do anything else. That is your opening to run in and hit it.
- **Getting hit:** every hit flashes it white and wobbles its shell. **Heavy hits** (20+ damage: the overhead slam and shots charged past about a third) knock it back, make it **hide in its shell** for a moment, and cancel its attack. Light hits (jab, bash, quick shots) do not interrupt it, and hitting an idle crab makes it chase you.
- **Shell hide:** if it watches you **charge a shot for half a second**, it tucks in and its bucket swings up off its back and **flips face down over it**, landing with a thump. Once the bucket is down, **bolts can't hurt it** from any direction: they bounce off with a clang and **fly back at you**, doing half their damage if they connect (with a slight wobble, so you can sometimes sidestep them). A shot that lands while the bucket is still flipping hits the crab and knocks it out of hiding. To beat it, walk up and **hit it with the laser**: melee knocks it out with full damage. Otherwise it stays hidden until you stop charging, then the bucket lifts back onto its back about 0.9 s later. Getting close does not bring it out. Quick shots fired before it reacts still get through.
- **Dead (120 HP, about three full combos or three strong shots):** it hops, flips onto its back, wiggles its legs, and sinks away. In this test scene it comes back where it started 5 s later.
- **Your death:** at 0 HP the screen shows `PINCHED! Respawning...`, and after 1.5 s you return to the start with full health. This is a **placeholder**; real death and respawn are sequence step 4.

## Shells

When the crab hides, its shell flips face down over it using the rig's **Hide Shell Position / Rotation**. While the shell is down the crab's own parts are switched off, so nothing pokes through, whatever the shell's shape.


The crab's **HermitCrabRig** component has a **Shell Prefab** slot. Any model can be a shell if its prefab follows one convention:

- The prefab's **origin** is the centre of the shell's opening, where the crab's body comes out.
- **+Z** points out of the opening (the way the crab faces), and **+Y** is up.

Arrange the model inside its prefab to match, like `MetalBucket.prefab`, which lies the bucket on its side with its mouth forward and handle on top. Shell colliders are removed when worn, because the crab's own capsule collider is used. **Shell Offset**, **Shell Rotation** and **Shell Scale** on the rig fine-tune one crab's shell. Code can swap shells with `HermitCrabRig.SetShell(prefab)`.

Outside Play mode the shell is drawn in the Scene view as a grey preview (Gizmos must be on). The real shell appears when the game starts.

## Settings

All values are placeholders, tunable on each crab's components in the Inspector.

**HermitCrab**

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Detect Range | 12 m | Notices you within this distance, with line of sight |
| Lose Range / Forget Time | 18 m / 3 s | Gives up beyond this distance, or after this long unseen |
| Wander Speed / Chase Speed | 1.2 / 3.2 m/s | Your walk is 4, sprint 7 |
| Wander Radius | 4 m | How far it ambles from home |
| Face Player While Chasing | on | Keeps facing you while chasing; off makes it scuttle sideways |
| Chase Turn Speed | 360°/s | How quickly it turns to keep facing you |
| Attack Range | 1.6 m | Starts an attack this close (centre to centre) |
| Attack Reach | 1.9 m | The claw connects if you are still this close and in front |
| Attack Arc | 90° | How wide the slam's hit area is in front of it; narrower makes sidestepping easier |
| Attack Damage | 15 | Damage per claw hit |
| Windup / Strike / Recover Time | 0.9 / 0.14 / 0.6 s | Claw raised and glowing (your warning), slam, recovery after a hit |
| Windup Tracking | 0.5 | Part of the wind-up it keeps aiming at you; after that its aim is locked |
| Miss Recover Time | 1.6 s | Recovery after a miss, with the claw stuck in the ground: your opening |
| Stumble Distance | 0.8 m | How far it is thrown backwards when it rips the claw free |
| Attack Cooldown | 0.7 s | Pause before it can attack again |
| Lunge Distance / Player Shove | 0.6 / 0.6 m | How far it lunges, and how far a hit pushes you |
| Stagger Threshold | 20 | Hits this strong knock it back, make it hide, and cancel its attack: the slam (20) and charged shots, not jabs or bashes (10) |
| Stagger Time | 0.9 s | How long it hides in its shell |
| Knockback | 1.1 m | Knockback for a 20-damage hit; scales with damage, up to double |
| Shell Guard | on | Hide and reflect bolts when it sees you charging |
| Hide After Charging | 0.5 s | How long it must watch you charge before it hides |
| Reflect Damage Multiplier | 0.5 | Damage a reflected bolt keeps when it flies back at you |
| Reflect Spread | 4° | Random wobble on a reflected bolt's aim |
| Guard Hold Time / Cooldown | 0.9 / 0.5 s | Stays hidden after you stop charging / gap before it can hide again |
| Corpse Time | 3.5 s | Time before a dead crab vanishes |
| Respawn Delay | 0 (5 in EnemyTest) | Test scenes only; 0 means it stays dead |

**HermitCrabRig**: Claw Glow Color sets the wind-up glow colour (default warm yellow-white). **Hide Shell Position / Rotation** set where the shell lands when flipped face down (defaults suit the bucket: mouth on the ground, rotated 105°), and **Shell Flip Time** (0.3 s) how long the flip takes. Other shells may need their own values.

**Health** on the crab: Max Health 120. **NavMeshAgent**: radius 0.5 m, height 0.9 m.

## Scripts

- `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/HermitCrab.cs`: the state machine (idle, chase, attack, stagger, shell hide, dead), bolt reflection while hiding (it is an **IBoltReflector**), line of sight, attacking the player's **Health**, reacting to hits through **Health** events, knockback, and the test respawn. It finds the player automatically.
- `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/HermitCrabRig.cs`: the crab's body. Procedural animation (scuttling legs, sideways turn, eye stalks, claw snaps, attack wind-up and slam, hiding, flinching, death flip), the white hit flash, and the swappable shell.
- `Assets/Scripts/Combat/IBoltReflector.cs` and `LaserBolt.cs`: laser bolts can now be bounced. A bolt that hits something implementing **IBoltReflector** asks it whether to reflect; if so, it flies off in the new direction, owned by the reflector, so it can hit whoever fired it. Nothing else reflects, so bolts behave as before everywhere else.
- `Assets/Scripts/Enemies/NavMeshBaker.cs`: builds the NavMesh from its **NavMeshSurface** when the scene starts, so no baked NavMesh asset needs committing. Bake in the editor instead for large levels.
- `Assets/Scripts/Player/PlaceholderRespawn.cs`: placeholder death and respawn for test scenes.
- `Assets/Scripts/Combat/CombatDebugHud.cs`: a new **Show Player Health** option (off by default, on in EnemyTest) shows the player's health, a red flash when hurt, and the respawn message. Other scenes, including the tutorial with its own health UI, are unchanged.
- `Assets/Editor/EnemyTestBuilder.cs`: one-time scene generator, prefab builder and validator.
- `Assets/Editor/LowPolyMesh.cs`: generates the faceted low-poly meshes (lathe for the bucket, icospheres, tapered limbs, the handle tube).

## Manual acceptance checks

1. The scene opens and enters Play Mode with no Console errors or warnings about the NavMesh. Movement and the mining laser work as in the combat test.
2. In the Scene view before pressing Play, each crab shows a grey bucket preview on its back. In Play mode both orange crabs wear grey metal buckets, lying on their sides with the crab peeking out of the mouth.
3. At the start, Crab A ambles around near its spot and ignores you. The HUD shows `HP 100 / 100` in the top left.
4. Walk toward Crab A. Within about 12 m it hops in surprise and starts chasing, always turned to face you, even when you circle around it. Its legs cycle and its eyes wobble.
5. Duck behind the pillar or crate stack: it loses sight. Stay hidden for a few seconds and it gives up and wanders back.
6. Let it reach you: it raises its big claw, which glows brighter and brighter, with a pulsing spark between the pincers. If you stay put, the claw slams down, the glow goes out, the screen flashes red, you are pushed back, and your HP drops by 15.
7. Wait until the claw is glowing brightly, then sidestep or back off: the slam misses and the claw sinks into the ground with a burst of dirt. The crab is clearly stuck: it heaves back on the claw again and again, legs scrabbling and eyes bulging, with dirt kicking up on each tug. Run in and hit it before it rips the claw free. When it does, it is thrown clearly backwards (about a body length), rearing up and wobbling with its eyes spinning.
8. Jab or bash it: it flashes white and its shell wobbles, but it keeps attacking. The HUD shows its health falling from 120.
9. Hit it with the overhead slam or a strong charged shot: it is knocked back, pulls into its bucket for a moment (legs, claws and eyes tuck in), and any attack in progress is cancelled.
10. Kill it: it hops, flips onto its back, wiggles, then sinks away. About 5 s later it returns at its starting spot with full health. The Console stays free of errors throughout.
11. From about 8 m, hold right click. About half a second in, the crab tucks in and its bucket swings up and flips face down over it, landing with a thump. No part of the crab shows under the bucket.
12. Release the shot: it clangs off the bucket with a spark and flies back at you. Try it from the side and from behind too: it bounces from any direction. The crab's health does not move. If the bolt hits you, the screen flashes red and you lose half the bolt's damage. Sidestep as soon as you fire to try to dodge it.
13. Walk right up to the hidden crab and stand next to it: it stays hidden. Jab it: full damage, the bucket lifts back onto its back, and the crab chases you.
14. Fire a quick tap shot before it has watched you charge for half a second: it hits normally.
15. Stop charging: about a second later the bucket lifts back onto its back and the crab chases you again.
16. Shoot an idle crab from far away: it starts chasing you even if it had not seen you.
17. Let the crabs reduce you to 0 HP: `PINCHED! Respawning...` appears, the crabs lose interest, and after 1.5 s you are back at the start with 100 HP and full control.
18. Both crabs can chase you at once without walking through each other, the walls, or the cover. They can follow you up the ramp onto the platform.
19. The combat test scene still works and shows no player health bar.
20. Exit and re-enter Play Mode, then repeat a quick fight.

## Validation performed

The scripts were written without a Unity editor. They compile cleanly against hand-written stand-ins for the Unity, NavMesh and editor APIs they use, which catches typos and type errors but is not a real Unity compile. The crab's layout was checked by rebuilding the same meshes and positions outside Unity and rendering them. Nothing has been compiled, generated or played in Unity yet. Scene generation and every check above remain for the first person to run this branch in Unity.
